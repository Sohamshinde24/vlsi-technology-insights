About Me

A Electronics and Computer Science student with a growing interest in VLSI (Very Large Scale Integration), Digital Electronics, and Semiconductor Technology.

I created this blog to explore and share my understanding of VLSI concepts in a simple and easy-to-understand way. Through this platform, I write about topics such as NMOS, PMOS, CMOS, Inverters, Logic Gates, Power Consumption, Performance, and Area Optimization.

My goal is to understand the concepts deeply while sharing useful technical knowledge with other students and beginners who are interested in VLSI and electronics.

This blog is also a part of my learning journey, where I connect theoretical concepts with practical applications in modern semiconductor and digital circuit design.
